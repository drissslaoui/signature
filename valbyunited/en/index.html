Who we are

More than a football club

Valby United is a vibrant community that brings people together across backgrounds, ages and ambitions. The club started in 2021 as Boldklubben Folehaven and took its current name in 2024, underlining the wish to include all of Valby.

For us, football is a tool for building togetherness and development for both elite and grassroots players. With dedicated coaches and a strong community, we welcome everyone to become part of our movement.

01

Inclusion

Everyone is welcome — whatever their background, age or level.

Dedicated coaches and an ambitious, structured approach on the pitch.
