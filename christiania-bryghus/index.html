Why hemp?

Not a gimmick.
A flavour.

  1. 01

    Same family tree

    Hops and hemp both belong to the Cannabaceae family. Brewed together they add earthy, herbal and resinous layers you won't find in an ordinary lager.

  2. 02

    A hint, or a clear taste

    Some of our beers carry just a whisper of hemp. Others wear it loud and proud. Every label tells you which is which.

  3. 03

    All flavour, no high

    We brew with food-grade hemp. It's about aroma and character, nothing else. The only buzz comes from the beer itself.

Brewed on Christiania

From kettle
to glass.

Small batches, brewed a few hundred metres from where you drink them. No shortcuts, no filters on the ideas.

  1. 01

    Mash

    Malted barley, warm water and patience. This is where body and colour are born.

  2. 02

    Boil

    Hops and hemp go in. Bitterness, aroma and that signature herbal edge come out.

  3. 03

    Ferment

    Yeast gets to work. Sometimes classic lager strains, sometimes wild Norwegian kveik.

  4. 04

    Pour

    Straight from our tanks to the taps at The Lab, and into bottles for the rest of Denmark.
